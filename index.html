To My Vasu, ❤️

Heyyy mera pyaara sa Vasu! 🥺❤️

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki humari story kitni randomly start hui thi. Tinder par tumhara woh random message, phir thodi der tak koi response nahi 😂, uske baad tumhara mera mobile number maangna, aur number exchange hone ke baad WhatsApp par tumhara mujhse Snap ID maangna, video call k liye 😁. Pata nahi tha ki ek random conversation meri life ka itna special part ban jayegi.

Aur ek baat pata hai? Mujhe October Theory yaad aati hai. 1st October ko maine ek story post ki thi, wishing for a better future (Be kind and nice to me october). Mujhe bilkul idea nahi tha ki usi din meri life mein koi aisa aane wala hai jo mere future ka itna beautiful part ban jayega. And that someone was YOU, Vasu! 🥺❤️

Phir usi raat humari first video call hui. Mujhe abhi bhi yaad hai tum mere eyes aur curly hairs ki tareef kar rahe the aur keh rahe the kitne cute hain. 😂❤️ Sach bataun, woh compliments mujhe aaj bhi yaad hain.

Aur phir humari first meeting! Pehle CP jaane ka plan banaya, lekin strike ki wajah se CP band tha. Phir Kashmere Gate aur finally Chandni Chowk! 😂 Wahan pehle Gurdwara Sis Ganj Sahib gaye, phir Red Fort ki taraf nikle.

Aur mujhe tumhara favourite pineapple bhi yaad hai! 🍍❤️ Pyaar sirf bade moments yaad rakhna nahi hota, kabhi-kabhi kisi ka favourite pineapple bhi yaad reh jaana hota hai. 🍍🥹❤️.

Aur woh jhuleeee! 😭😂 Dono bade brave ban rahe the, lekin jaise hi ride top par pahunchi, humari hawa tight ho gayi. Dar ke maare ek dusre ko hug kar rahe the. Honestly, woh moment kitna cute tha! aur vo 2 ladkiya kaise dekh rhi thi k j kya ho rha hai bhai 😳🤣

Aur Gauri Shankar Mandir ki jagah galti se Jain Mandir pahunch jaana. 😂 Phir apni galti realise karke hasna aur finally correct mandir jaana. Pata nahi kyun, lekin humari yeh chhoti-chhoti mistakes bhi meri favourite memories ban gayi hain.

Lekin sabse special baat yeh hai ki dheere-dheere humne ek dusre ke saath apne secrets, feelings aur woh baatein share karni shuru ki jo shayad hum kisi aur ko nahi bata paate. Aur jab pata chala ki humare secrets bhi kitne similar hain, toh sach mein laga ki God ne kuch toh special kiya hai humare liye. ❤️

Ek baat aur, jab tumne apna secret batakar kaha tha ki tum piche hat rhe ho 💔😭 so that k meri vjah se tumhe koi problem na ho hum friends bankar continue kar sakte hain, honestly uss waqt mujhe bahut bura laga tha. Kyunki main tumhe sirf friend bankar nahi dekhna chahta tha kinda selfish at that moment. Mujhe darr tha ki kahin koi aur tumhari life mein na aa jaaye aur main tumhe kho na doon.

Aur mere overthinker baby, mujhe pata hai tum bhi kabhi-kabhi mujhe lose karne se darte ho. 🥺 But please apne mind mein itni negative stories mat banaya karo. Jab bhi kuch bother kare, mujhse baat karna. Main chahta hoon ki hum ek dusre ko samjhein, na ki apne thoughts mein hi pareshaan hote rahein.

Mujhe pata hai hum perfect nahi hain. Kabhi misunderstandings hongi, kabhi arguments, kabhi ek dusre ko irritate bhi karenge. 😂 But mujhe perfect relationship nahi chahiye, mujhe humara relationship chahiye, jisme pyaar ho, hasi ho, thoda sa pagalpan ho aur bahut saari memories hon.

Bas aage bhi aise hi random plans banane hain, dates par jaana hai, pictures leni hain, ek dusre ko hug karna hai aur naye-naye memories create karni hain. Haan, next time correct mandir hi jaana hai please! 😭😂

Aur jab bhi Enna Sona suno, toh humari story yaad karna. Ek random Tinder message se lekar yahan tak ka safar kitna unexpected aur beautiful raha hai na?

Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye, Vasu. Kabhi-kabhi lagta hai meri 1st October wali wish ka answer tum the. ❤️ I don't know future mein kya-kya hoga, but I genuinely want ki hum aur bhi bahut saari beautiful memories banayein.

I love you so much, mere pyaare overthinker. Kabhi doubt mat karna ki tum mere liye kitne special ho. ❤️

Ab jaldi se ek tight hug do apne boy ko. No excuses! 🫂💋

Forever yours,
Tumhara favourite headache, Jaggu🙈❤️

P.S. Thank God Tinder par tumne message kiya tha, warna mujhe un scary jhulon par hug karne wala kaun milta? 😂❤️
